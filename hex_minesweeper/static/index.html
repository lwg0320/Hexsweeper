HOW TO PLAY

Simple rules, six directions.

  • 01

    Reveal a cell. Your first reveal is always safe.

  • 02

    A number tells you how many mines touch that hex.

  • 03

    Flag suspected mines. Reveal every safe cell to win.

Click to reveal · Right-click to flag · On touch screens, turn on Flag mode.

OPTIONAL ASSISTANCE

Choose an agent.
