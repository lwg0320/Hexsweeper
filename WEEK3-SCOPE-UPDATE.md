> **Scope change:** I am replacing the Poolshark reconstruction direction with Hexsweeper, a small single-player Minesweeper adaptation on a hex grid, implemented from scratch. Each episode uses a seeded small, medium, or large hex map; the player reveals and flags cells, gets a safe first reveal, and wins by revealing every safe cell or loses by revealing a mine. The episode is bounded by explicit runner limits. The game omits timers, chording, power-ups, and high scores. If I need to cut scope further, I will remove the small and large presets first and keep the medium-board loop and observation/action interface.
